Hackwize is an open-source collection of JavaScript bookmarklets designed to remove the restrictions and surveillance imposed by Linewize on school networks. By running entirely in your browser — no installs, no extensions — each tool lets students access blocked websites and reclaim an unrestricted learning environment with a single click.

## What Is Linewize?

Linewize is a network filtering and monitoring platform commonly deployed by schools to control which websites students can visit and to log their browsing activity. While it is intended to enforce acceptable-use policies, it frequently blocks legitimate educational resources and introduces unwanted surveillance. Hackwize targets Linewize's filtering layer directly, exploiting its own mechanisms to disable or route around it.

## What Hackwize Provides

Hackwize ships five tools covering different bypass strategies, from a one-click filter killer to a full floating proxy menu:

  <Card title="Linewize Killer" icon="ban" href="/tools/linewize-killer">

  <Card title="Proxies Menu" icon="bars" href="/tools/proxies-menu">
