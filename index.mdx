Hackwize is an open-source collection of bookmarklets and mods that remove the restrictions imposed by Linewize — the network filtering software used in many schools. Whether you need to bypass content filters, access proxies, or hack the i-Ready learning platform, Hackwize has a tool for you.

## Get Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a tool">
    Browse the [Hacks & Tools](/tools/linewize-killer) section to find the right bookmarklet for your use case.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the code">
    Each tool page contains the full bookmarklet code ready to copy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to your browser">
    Follow the [Installing Bookmarklets](/guides/installing-bookmarklets) guide to add it to your browser's bookmarks bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click and run">
    Navigate to the target site and click the bookmarklet to activate it.
  </Step>
</Steps>
