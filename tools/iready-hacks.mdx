The I-Ready Hacks bookmarklet (jVoid Revamp) is an extensive JavaScript hack suite targeting the i-Ready adaptive learning platform used in schools for adaptive reading and math curriculum. At over 600KB, it is by far the largest and most feature-rich tool in the Hackwize collection, delivering a broad range of platform modifications and bypasses through a single bookmarklet click.

## What It Targets

[i-Ready](https://www.curriculumassociates.com/programs/i-ready-learning) is a widely deployed adaptive learning platform covering reading and mathematics, used across K–8 school districts. The I-Ready Hacks bookmarklet runs inside an active i-Ready session and applies modifications at the JavaScript level within the page's own execution environment.

## Size and Obfuscation

The bookmarklet weighs in at approximately **626KB** of heavily obfuscated JavaScript. The obfuscation is intentional — it makes the code difficult to detect, analyse, or block by automated content filters and platform integrity checks. Variable names are replaced with encoded identifiers (e.g. `_0x57f8bc`, `_0x335899`) and string literals are stored in a rotating lookup array resolved at runtime.
